## Terminal mapping

This source is Quattro-only and supplies no `alacritty.toml`. The 16 ANSI slots follow Omarchy’s template at:
https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/blob/b679363bed05415771a1b1dc92c6899a908236f7/default/themed/alacritty.toml.tpl

Normal black/white map to `background`/`foreground`; bright black/white to `muted`/`bright_foreground`; the six named hues use their corresponding normal/bright fields.
